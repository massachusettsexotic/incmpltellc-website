Mom's Heat Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Mom's Heat is a heating calculator for a large old house. You set the thermostats, the outdoor temperature and a few prices, and it shows how warm each floor gets, where the heat goes, and what it costs. This policy explains what the app does (and does not do) with information on your device.

TL;DR

  • Mom's Heat does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to the developer or to anyone else.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers, and has no user accounts.
  • It works entirely offline. It does not use the internet, your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or Bluetooth.

Information stored on your device

The app remembers one thing: whether you have already seen its welcome screen. The numbers you type in (thermostats, prices) are used on the screen only. Nothing is sent to Incmplte LLC or any server. Deleting the app deletes everything.

Children

Mom's Heat is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
