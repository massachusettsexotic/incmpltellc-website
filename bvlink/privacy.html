BV Link Demo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

BV Link Demo is a demonstration app. Every site, sensor, reading and alarm in it is simulated on your device. It does not connect to any real equipment.

TL;DR

  • The app does not collect, store or send any personal information to the developer or to anyone else.
  • It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party trackers.
  • It does not use the internet, Bluetooth, location, contacts or photos.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows simulated alarms as local notifications created on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Values you type in

Offsets and lab values you enter in the demo stay in memory on your device and are discarded when the app closes.
