Avocado Tester Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Avocado Tester watches avocados ripen. The avocado sits in a small tester under a closed lid, a probe gently presses it on a schedule, and reports how much it gives; the app tracks the softening and lets you know when the avocado is ripe. This policy explains what the app does (and does not do) with information on your device. It applies to the iPhone and Android versions.

TL;DR

  • Avocado Tester does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to the developer or to anyone else.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers, and has no user accounts.
  • Your avocados, readings, notes, photos and settings stay on your device unless you choose to export them.
  • Bluetooth and your local Wi-Fi network are used only to talk to your own Avocado Tester probes.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following in its local storage on your device. None of it is sent to Incmplte LLC or any server.

  • Avocados and readings. Each avocado's name, variety, size, where it's kept, optional photo and notes, and every probe reading (how far the probe travelled, temperature, battery, time).
  • Your ratings. If you tell the app how an avocado turned out ("I ate it"), it uses that to adjust your ripeness settings — on your device only.
  • Probes and settings. Probe names, the local network address a probe announces, alert and quiet-hour settings, units.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Bluetooth and local network

The app asks for Bluetooth access (on Android, the "Nearby devices" permission) solely to find and connect to Avocado Tester probes. When a probe reports its address on your home Wi-Fi, the app may talk to it directly over your local network. It does not scan for, record, or report any other device, and readings go only from your probe to your device.

Photos

If you add a photo of an avocado, you pick it with the system photo picker; the app only receives the photo you choose and keeps it on your device.

Notifications

Ripeness alerts are local notifications created on your device. No notification service or server is involved.

Exports

You can export readings as a CSV file with your device's share sheet. That only happens when you start it, and the file goes only where you send it.

Children

Avocado Tester is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
