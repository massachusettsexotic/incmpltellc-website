Avocado Tester

Stop squeezing. It'll tell you.

Clamp a little probe onto an avocado and leave it there. On a schedule it gives the fruit a gentle press, just like your thumb, measures how much it gives, and lets go. Your phone keeps the chart and pings you when it's turning, when it's ripe, and when it's time to eat it today.

Each avocado is its own yardstick

There's no guessing what "ripe" means for every fruit. The first press is that avocado's baseline, and the app watches how it softens from there, with an honest estimate like "ready in about a day".

It learns what you like

Tell it how each avocado turned out, a little firm, perfect, or too far, and it adjusts your ripe point to match.

iPhone and Android. No account, no ads, no tracking, and everything stays on your phone.
