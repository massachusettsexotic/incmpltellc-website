BevSense Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

BevSense is a carbonation measurement app for beverage quality control. It reads pressure and temperature from a BevSense Bluetooth probe and calculates dissolved CO₂. This policy explains what the app does (and does not do) with information on your device. It applies to the iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android versions.

TL;DR

  • BevSense does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to the developer or to anyone else.
  • BevSense does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers, and has no user accounts.
  • Everything you enter or record — readings, operator name, location labels, calibration settings — stays on your device unless you choose to export it.
  • Bluetooth is used only to talk to a BevSense probe nearby.

Information stored on your device

BevSense stores the following in the app's local storage on your device. None of it is sent to BevSense, Incmplte LLC, or any server.

  • Readings. Pressure, temperature, calculated CO₂ volumes, the time of each reading, and any operator name, location or sample notes you type in.
  • Settings. Units, specification limits, calibration gain and offset, and the name of the last probe you connected to.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Bluetooth

The app asks for Bluetooth access (on Android, the “Nearby devices” permission) solely to find and connect to a BevSense probe. It does not scan for, record, or report any other device. Readings travel directly from the probe to your device and nowhere else.

Exports

You can export readings as a PDF report or a CSV file using your device's share sheet. That only happens when you start it, and the file goes only where you send it. The app never uploads exports on its own.

Network

BevSense makes no network requests of its own. The BevSense contact screen contains ordinary links to a phone number, email address and website; tapping one opens the corresponding app on your device.

Children

BevSense is a professional tool and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
