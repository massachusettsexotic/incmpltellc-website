BoatWatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

BoatWatch keeps an eye on a boat through a small unit you own aboard: bilge pumps, high water, batteries and shore power. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

TL;DR

  • BoatWatch does not collect, store or send any personal information to the developer or to anyone else.
  • No accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party trackers.
  • Your boats, readings and settings stay on your device.

Information stored on your device

  • Boats. The names you give them and the network address of your BoatWatch unit.
  • Readings and alerts. Bilge pump activity, water, battery and charger status reported by your own unit, and your alert settings.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Bluetooth and local network

The app uses Bluetooth and your local network only to find and talk to your own BoatWatch unit. It does not scan for, record or report any other device.

Notifications

Alerts are local notifications created on your device.
