Port Things Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Port Things is a travel guide app holding offline guides to cruise ports and destinations around the world. This policy explains what Port Things does (and does not do) with information on your device.

TL;DR

  • Port Things does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you.
  • Port Things does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers.
  • Everything you create in the app — group members, schedule, favorites — is stored only on your device.
  • The app makes anonymous network requests to Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons, Mapillary, Apple Maps, and venue websites to load photos and imagery. These services have their own privacy policies.

Information stored on your device

Port Things stores the following on your device, in your iPhone's local app storage. None of it is transmitted to Port Things, the developer, or any third-party server.

  • Group members. Names, ages, emojis, mobility/access settings, interests, and any notes you choose to add.
  • Schedule items. Venues you've added to your trip plan, dates, optional times, and notes.
  • Favorites. Venues you've starred.
  • Language preference. Your chosen interface language.
  • Photo cache. Cached image URLs and image bytes the app has fetched. Stored in your app's Caches directory.

To delete all of this data, delete the Port Things app from your device.

Network requests

Port Things makes the following anonymous network requests to populate venue content. None of these requests include personally identifying information about you, your account, or your device beyond what the operating system and the remote service automatically observe (your IP address).

  • Wikipedia API (en.wikipedia.org) — to look up venue articles and thumbnail images. Operated by the Wikimedia Foundation. See the Wikimedia Privacy Policy.
  • Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org, upload.wikimedia.org) — to find and download geotagged venue photos. Same Wikimedia policy.
  • Mapillary (graph.mapillary.com) — to load street-level photos taken near a venue. Operated by Mapillary (a Meta company). See the Mapillary Privacy Policy. Requests contain only the venue's coordinates, never yours.
  • Venue websites — to fetch a venue's public preview image (the same image the venue publishes for link sharing). The request goes to that venue's own website.
  • Apple Look Around and Apple Maps — to load street-level imagery, satellite snapshots, and map tiles. Operated by Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Translation framework — on-device translation. No text is sent to Apple servers; translation happens locally on your iPhone.
  • Linked websites — when you tap "Visit Website" or "Read on Wikipedia", iOS opens the link in your browser. That browser handles the request, not Port Things.

Port Things has no servers of its own. There is no Port Things backend that sees your usage.

Purchases

Port Things is a paid app that includes two guides of your choice. You can buy more guides, cruise-region packs or all guides inside the app. Every purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details; the app only receives Apple's confirmation of which guides you own.

Your included guides and iCloud

So your two included guides survive a reinstall or a new iPhone, the app saves which guides you picked in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. That record contains only guide names and the date you picked them. It lives in your iCloud account, and we cannot read it.

Photo downloads

When you own a guide, its photos download from Apple's servers (App Store on-demand resources) and are kept on your device so they work at sea. No personal information is sent with these downloads.

Optional location

Port Things can optionally access your device location to sort venues by distance ("nearest first"). This is purely optional and uses iOS's standard location-permission dialog.

  • Location data is used only on your device to compute distances.
  • Location data is never transmitted off your device.
  • You can deny location permission and the app will work normally; venues will just be shown without distance sorting.
  • You can revoke location permission anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Port Things.

Children

Port Things is a travel guide for groups, which may include children. Children of any age can be added as group members. No information about anyone you add to a group is ever transmitted off your device. Port Things is not directed specifically at children and does not knowingly collect information from children, because it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because Port Things does not collect or store any data about you on any server, there is no account to delete and no data export to request. To delete the data you have created in the app, delete the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how Port Things handles information, the updated policy will be posted at this URL and the "Last updated" date above will change. Substantive changes will also be noted in app release notes.
