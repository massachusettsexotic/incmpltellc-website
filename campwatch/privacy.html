CampWatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

CampWatch keeps an eye on a camp, cabin or other property through a small watcher unit you own: inside temperature, power, water and doors. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

TL;DR

  • CampWatch does not collect, store or send any personal information to the developer or to anyone else.
  • No accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party trackers.
  • Your properties, readings and settings stay on your device.

Information stored on your device

  • Properties. The names you give them, an optional address or place you type in, and the network address of your watcher unit.
  • Readings and alerts. Temperatures, power, water and door status reported by your own unit, and your alert settings.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Bluetooth and local network

The app uses Bluetooth and your local network only to find and talk to your own CampWatch unit or hub. It does not scan for, record or report any other device.

Weather

If you add a place for a property, the app asks Apple Maps to turn it into map coordinates and asks Apple Weather for the outdoor forecast there. Those requests go to Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the app does not use your own location.

Notifications

Alerts are local notifications created on your device.
